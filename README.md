# 语音对话机器人 使用手册

## 开始对话

1. 打开页面后，点击底部的**绿色接听按钮**
2. 听到提示后，直接对着麦克风说话
3. 说完后 AI 会自动识别并语音回复
4. 想结束对话，点击**红色挂断按钮**即可

---

## 打断 AI

AI 说话过程中，你可以**直接开口说话**，AI 会立刻停止并重新听你说。

---

## 调整麦克风灵敏度

点击右上角的**设置图标**，可以看到麦克风阈值滑块：

- **滑块拉到最左（0）**：最灵敏，适合安静环境
- **滑块向右调高**：过滤背景噪音，适合嘈杂环境
- 点击**重置**可恢复默认

> 如果你说话没有反应，尝试把滑块调回最左边。

---

## 常见问题

**说话没有任何反应？**
检查浏览器是否弹出了麦克风授权提示，点击"允许"即可。

**AI 没有听清我说的话？**
靠近麦克风说话，或将灵敏度滑块调回最左边。

**背景噪音太多，AI 频繁误触发？**
将灵敏度滑块适当向右调高，过滤掉背景声音。

# Voice Chatbot User Guide

## Starting a Conversation

1. Open the page and tap the **green answer button** at the bottom
2. Speak directly into your microphone
3. The AI will automatically recognize your speech and reply with voice
4. To end the conversation, tap the **red hang-up button**

---

## Interrupting the AI

While the AI is speaking, you can **start talking at any time** — the AI will stop immediately and listen to you.

---

## Adjusting Microphone Sensitivity

Tap the **settings icon** in the top right corner to find the microphone threshold slider:

- **Slider all the way left (0)**: Most sensitive, best for quiet environments
- **Slider moved right**: Filters out background noise, best for noisy environments
- Tap **Reset** to restore the default setting

> If the AI doesn't respond when you speak, try moving the slider back to the far left.

---

## FAQ

**No response when I speak?**
Check if your browser showed a microphone permission prompt and make sure you clicked "Allow".

**The AI can't understand me clearly?**
Move closer to the microphone, or move the sensitivity slider back to the far left.

**Too much background noise causing the AI to trigger by mistake?**
Move the sensitivity slider to the right to filter out background sounds.
